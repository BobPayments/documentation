---
title: "Create checkout session"
openapi: "POST /api/v1/checkout-sessions/"
---

Create a session on your backend and redirect the buyer to the returned `checkoutUrl`. In `paymentMethods`, use `['pix']` (the default), `['crypto']`, `['credit_card']`, or combinations — only methods enabled on the project.

<Warning>
  Never create a session in the frontend with a `sk_test_` or `sk_live_` key. This endpoint requires a secret key and must be called from your server.
</Warning>
